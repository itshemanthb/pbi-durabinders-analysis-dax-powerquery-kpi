
# 📊 DuraBinders Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

### 1. Project Title / Headline

📈 **DuraBinders Sales Analytics: Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional sales distribution. The report provides a comprehensive view of total sales, total profit, orders, and customers, helping users explore business performance and identify trends across products, markets, and geographic regions.

### 2. Short Description / Purpose

The DuraBinders Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report developed to monitor business performance and analyze sales data across products, customers, markets, and regions. It includes key performance indicators such as total sales, total profit, total orders, and total customers, along with visualizations for sales trends, product performance, geographic distribution, and customer segmentation.

The report consists of four pages: Total Sales Overview, Regional Performance Analysis, Product Category Insights, and Sales by Customer Segments. These pages allow users to explore sales performance from an executive perspective and drill into specific product categories, geographic markets, and customer groups to support data-driven decision-making.

### 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main business intelligence platform used to create the interactive dashboard and reports.
- 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data preparation, transformation, and cleaning before analysis.
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create measures for total sales, total profit, total orders, total customers, previous-month performance, and other analytical calculations.
- 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Uses sales, calendar, and measure-related data structures to support analysis across multiple business dimensions.
- 📈 **Interactive Visualizations** – KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, donut charts, maps, decomposition trees, and tables.
- 🌎 **Geographic Analysis** – Azure Maps and geographic fields used to visualize sales distribution across regions and countries.
- 🎛️ **Power BI Interactivity** – Interactive visuals, page navigation buttons, and cross-filtering to explore different areas of the report.
- 🖼️ **Custom Dashboard Design** – Branded DuraBinders logo, custom icons, and visual elements used to create a consistent report layout.
- 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for the Power BI project and `.png` for dashboard screenshots and previews.

### 4. Data Source

**Source: Sales and Customer Analytics Dataset**

The dashboard uses a structured sales dataset organized around orders, products, customers, geographic locations, and customer segments.

The report includes the following analytical fields:

- **Orders and Sales** – Sales amounts, order quantities, and order-related information.
- **Profitability** – Profit values used to evaluate financial performance across products and customer segments.
- **Product Information** – Product names, categories, and subcategories used for product-level analysis.
- **Customer Information** – Customer names and customer-related sales metrics.
- **Geographic Information** – Country, state, region, and market fields used for regional sales analysis.
- **Customer Segmentation** – Segment classifications used to compare sales, profit, and order performance across customer groups.
- **Calendar Information** – Date, year, quarter, and month fields used for time-based analysis.
- **Performance Measures** – Total sales, total profit, total orders, total customers, previous-month comparisons, and related analytical metrics.

These data elements allow users to examine business performance from different perspectives and investigate sales trends across products, locations, and customer segments.

### 5. Features / Highlights

**• Business Problem**

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data across products, customers, markets, and geographic locations. Analyzing this information through raw tables can make it difficult to monitor overall performance, identify profitable products, understand customer segments, and evaluate regional sales contributions.

Important business questions include:

- What are the total sales, profit, orders, and customers?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which countries, states, and markets contribute the most sales?
- Which product categories and subcategories generate the highest revenue?
- Which products contribute most to total profit?
- Which customer segments generate the most sales and orders?
- How do customer segments compare in terms of sales and profitability?
- Which customers contribute significantly to overall sales?

The dashboard addresses these questions through interactive visualizations and consolidated business performance indicators.

**• Goal of the Dashboard**

To develop an interactive business intelligence solution that:

- Provides a centralized overview of key business performance indicators.
- Tracks total sales, profit, orders, and customers.
- Analyzes sales performance over time.
- Identifies high-performing products, categories, and subcategories.
- Compares sales performance across markets and geographic regions.
- Evaluates customer segments based on sales, profit, and order volume.
- Supports detailed analysis through interactive maps, charts, and tables.
- Enables data-driven decisions related to sales strategy, product performance, and customer management.

**• Walkthrough of Key Visuals**

- **Total Sales Overview – Executive Dashboard**

  Provides a high-level summary of business performance using KPI cards for total sales, total profit, total orders, and total customers. The page includes sales trend analysis, monthly performance comparisons, category-level order analysis, and a table of top-performing products.

- **Total Sales (KPI Card)**

  Displays total sales revenue across the available dataset, providing a quick overview of overall business performance.

- **Total Profit (KPI Card)**

  Shows the total profit generated by sales, helping users evaluate the overall profitability of the business.

- **Total Orders (KPI Card)**

  Displays the total number of orders, providing an overview of transaction activity.

- **Total Customers (KPI Card)**

  Shows the total customer count according to the report's customer measure, helping users understand the size of the customer base.

- **Sales Trending (Line Chart)**

  Displays sales performance over time, enabling users to identify growth patterns, fluctuations, and changes across reporting periods.

- **Orders by Category (Bar Chart)**

  Compares order volumes across product categories to highlight categories with higher demand.

- **Monthly Sales vs Previous Month (KPI Visual)**

  Compares sales performance with the previous month's measure, supporting month-over-month performance monitoring.

- **Monthly Orders vs Previous Month (KPI Visual)**

  Compares current order performance with the previous month's measure, helping users monitor changes in transaction volume.

- **Monthly Profit vs Previous Month (KPI Visual)**

  Compares profit performance with the previous month's measure, providing insight into changes in profitability.

- **Top 10 Products (Table)**

  Displays leading products alongside total orders, sales, profit, and customer-related measures. This visual supports product-level performance comparisons.

- **Regional Performance Analysis**

  Provides a geographic view of sales performance using country-level and state-level visualizations, market comparisons, and a geographic map.

- **Sales by Country (Donut Chart)**

  Shows the distribution of sales across countries, helping users compare the contribution of different national markets.

- **Sales by State (Donut Chart)**

  Displays sales distribution across states, allowing users to examine regional differences in business performance.

- **Sales by Market (Bar Chart)**

  Compares total sales across markets, helping users identify markets that contribute significantly to overall revenue.

- **Geographic Sales Distribution (Azure Maps)**

  Visualizes sales across geographic regions and countries. The map helps users explore where sales are concentrated and understand the geographic distribution of business activity.

- **Product Category Insights**

  Focuses on product performance and profitability using category comparisons, hierarchical analysis, and subcategory-level profit distribution.

- **Sales by Category (Column Chart)**

  Compares total sales across product categories, helping users identify the categories that contribute most to revenue.

- **Sales Decomposition Tree**

  Allows users to break down total sales by category, subcategory, and individual product name. This interactive visual supports hierarchical exploration and helps users investigate the factors contributing to overall sales.

- **Profit by Subcategory (Treemap)**

  Displays total profit across product subcategories, using visual area to help compare the relative contribution of different subcategories to profitability.

- **Sales by Customer Segments**

  Provides an overview of sales performance across customer segments using KPI cards, customer-level tables, combo charts, and donut charts.

- **Customer Sales and Quantity (Table)**

  Displays customer names alongside sales and quantity measures, allowing users to compare individual customer contributions.

- **Sales and Profit by Segment (Combo Chart)**

  Combines total sales and total profit by customer segment, enabling users to compare revenue generation and profitability across different customer groups.

- **Profit by Segment (Donut Chart)**

  Displays the distribution of total profit across customer segments, helping users understand which segments contribute to overall profitability.

- **Orders by Segment (Donut Chart)**

  Compares total orders across customer segments, providing insight into transaction distribution among different customer groups.

- **Interactive Navigation**

  Navigation buttons allow users to move between the Total Sales Overview, Regional Performance Analysis, Product Category Insights, and Sales by Customer Segments pages.

**• Business Impact & Insights**

- **Sales Performance Monitoring:** Track total sales, profit, orders, and customers through a centralized dashboard.
- **Regional Analysis:** Compare sales across countries, states, regions, and markets to understand geographic performance.
- **Product Optimization:** Identify high-performing product categories and subcategories to support product strategy.
- **Profitability Analysis:** Examine profit contributions across products and customer segments.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Compare sales, profit, and order activity across different customer groups.
- **Customer Performance Analysis:** Identify customers with significant sales contributions and explore customer-level performance.
- **Trend Analysis:** Monitor sales and profitability changes over time to support business planning.
- **Performance Comparison:** Use previous-month KPI comparisons to understand changes in sales, order volume, and profit.
- **Data-Driven Decision-Making:** Transform raw sales data into interactive visual insights that support business analysis and strategic planning.
